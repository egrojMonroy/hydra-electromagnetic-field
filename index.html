Electromagnetic Field

3D magnetic dipoles for Hydra: microphone energy drives field amount; magnets move on a 4‑minute loop (cluster → split → orbit → two groups).

Open in Hydra Source on GitHub

Controls

  • 1–4 — active magnets
  • Mouse — tilt the field
  • Mic — field strength / density (allow when prompted)
